#1

leukaemia

▼
n. 白血病
A type of malignancy affecting the blood cells or blood-forming tissues.
📚 讲解与辨析 (Explanation)
  • 💡 作为名词：造血组织的任何特定形式或类型的癌症。
🔗 常见搭配 (Collocations)
  • Personal history of leukaemia (白血病个人史)
  • Acute Myeloid Leukaemia (白血病)
  • Acute leukaemia (急性白血病)
  • leukaemia cell (白血病细胞)
  • Chronic Myeloid Leukaemia (白血病)
📰 真实语境 (In Context)
"The subjects suffered from lymphoblastic and myeloid leukemias."

#2

headlight

▼
n. 汽车等的前灯；前灯的光束；船上的桅灯；矿工和医生用的帽灯
A bright light, with a lens and reflector, on the front of a motor vehicle (or originally a ship or train), designed to illuminate the road when driving at night; normally one of a pair.
📚 讲解与辨析 (Explanation)
  • 💡 作为名词：（主要是复数）女人的乳房。
  • 💡 作为名词：（主要是复数）女人直立的乳头，部分被衣服遮住。
近义词 (Synonyms)
  • headlamp (头灯)
🔗 常见搭配 (Collocations)
  • Headlight nest (前照灯灯座)
  • Headlight aimers (前照灯调准器)
  • Headlight wiper control (前照灯刮水器操纵件)
  • Headlight tester (前照灯试验器)
  • HEADLIGHT DISCO (公路迪斯科)

#4

crammed

▼
adj. 挤满的；塞满的；v. 把……塞进，挤满；（为应考）临时死记硬背（cram 的过去式和过去分词）
To press, force, or drive, particularly in filling, or in thrusting one thing into another; to stuff; to fill to superfluity.
📚 讲解与辨析 (Explanation)
  • 💡 作为动词：用食物填饱；去东西。
  • 💡 作为动词：匆匆完成大量的记忆或学习过程，如准备考试。
🔗 常见搭配 (Collocations)
  • cram school (补习班；补习学校，强化学校)
  • cram for (临时抱佛脚；填鸭式地学习；死记硬背)
📰 真实语境 (In Context)

▼
n. 小女孩，姑娘；<苏格兰>女佣；【名】 （Lass）（德、俄、捷）拉斯（人名）

🔗 常见搭配 (Collocations)

  • Lass deine Seele fliegen (就让你的灵魂飞翔)
  • Lass Diarra (拉斯)
  • Fella and Lass (菲儿莱丝)
  • Lass dich fallen (躺下吧)
