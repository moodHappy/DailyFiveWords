▼

#4

cast iron

▼
[材] 铸铁，生铁，锻铁
A hard and brittle, but strong, alloy of iron, carbon, and silicon, formed by casting in a mould.
📚 讲解与辨析 (Explanation)
  • 💡 作为形容词：由铸铁制成。
  • 💡 作为形容词：耐用；艰难的;有弹性。
近义词 (Synonyms)
  • iron (铁)
  • unrelenting (不屈不挠)
  • impervious (不透水)
  • iron-clad (钢铁般的)
  • unshakable (不可动摇的)
🔗 常见搭配 (Collocations)

#5

poppy

▼
n. 罂粟花；罂粟属植物；深红色；adj. 罂粟科的
Any plant of the genus Papaver or the family Papaveraceae, with crumpled, often red, petals and a milky juice having narcotic properties; especially the common poppy or corn poppy (Papaver rhoeas) which has orange-red flowers; the flower of such a plant.
📚 讲解与辨析 (Explanation)
  • 💡 作为名词：带有橙色的鲜红色，就像常见的罂粟花一样。
  • 💡 作为名词：戴在纽扣孔中或在其他场合展示的简单人造罂粟花，以纪念在两次世界大战和其他武装冲突中丧生的人们，特别是在阵亡将士纪念日前后。
🔗 常见搭配 (Collocations)
  • opium poppy (n. 罂粟)
  • poppy seed (罂粟籽)
