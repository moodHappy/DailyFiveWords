#1

reparation

▼
n. 赔偿；修理；赔款
(usually in the plural) A payment of time, effort or money to undo past transgression(s).
📚 讲解与辨析 (Explanation)
  • 💡 作为名词：更新、恢复等行为，或更新或修复的状态。
近义词 (Synonyms)
  • compensation (赔偿)
  • restoration (恢复)
  • restitution (归还)
  • amends (修正)
  • satisfaction (满意)
🔗 常见搭配 (Collocations)
  • war reparation (战争赔款)
  • DNA reparation (DNA修复)
  • reparation payment (赔款支付)
  • moral reparation (精神赔偿)
  • pecuniary reparation (金钱赔偿)
📰 真实语境 (In Context)
"the reparation of a bridge or of a highway"

percussion

▼
n. 打击乐器，敲击乐器；（管弦乐团的）打击乐器组（the percussion）；敲击，撞击；叩诊
The collision of two bodies in order to produce a sound.
📚 讲解与辨析 (Explanation)
  • 💡 作为名词：如此产生的声音。
  • 💡 作为名词：枪支中雷帽的爆炸。
🔗 常见搭配 (Collocations)
  • percussion instrument (打击乐器)
  • percussion drilling (冲击钻探，冲魂；冲唤钻眼，冲魂井)

#3

misguided

▼
adj. 被误导的；v. 使入歧途（misguide 的过去分词）
To guide poorly or incorrectly.
📚 讲解与辨析 (Explanation)
  • 💡 作为动词：引入歧途；导致错误。
  • 💡 作为形容词：考虑不周或没有经过深思熟虑
近义词 (Synonyms)
  • confused (困惑)
  • mislead (误导)
  • course (课程)
  • straying (迷路)
  • off (离开)
🔗 常见搭配 (Collocations)
  • Misguided jealousy (呷错醋)
  • Misguided love (冤枉相思)
  • misguided d (误导的)
  • Misguided Rage (歧途怒火)
  • misguided a (误入歧途的)
📰 真实语境 (In Context)
"A misguided child may use his personal power in mischievous ways"

#4

alight

▼
adj. 燃烧着的；闪亮的；（兴奋得）神采飞扬的；v. （鸟或虫等）飞落；从（公共汽车、火车等）下来
(with from) To get off or exit a vehicle or animal; to descend; to dismount.
📚 讲解与辨析 (Explanation)
  • 💡 作为动词：（与 on 或 at 连用）下降、定居、停留、休息或停止。
  • 💡 作为动词：（后接 on）偶然发现；偶然发现；来吧。
🔗 常见搭配 (Collocations)
  • set alight (使燃烧，使着火)
📰 真实语境 (In Context)
"He alighted from his horse."

#5

intermittent

▼
adj. 间歇的，断断续续的
An intermittent fever or disease.
📚 讲解与辨析 (Explanation)
  • 💡 作为形容词：间歇性地停止和开始、发生或呈现；在特定时间跨度之后出现。
  • 💡 作为形容词：（具体来说，水体的）仅在某些季节存在；也就是说，一年中有部分时间是干旱的。
近义词 (Synonyms)
  • periodic (定期)
  • spasmodic (痉挛)
  • periodical (期刊)
  • patchy (斑驳的)
反义词 (Antonyms)
  • steady (稳定)
  • constant (常数)
  • continual (连续的)
🔗 常见搭配 (Collocations)
  • intermittent operation (间歇式操作，间歇运行)
  • intermittent claudication ([医]间歇性跛行)
  • intermittent production (间歇生产)
📰 真实语境 (In Context)
"Intermittent bugs are most difficult to reproduce."
